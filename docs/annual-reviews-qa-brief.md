# Two annual reviews are now in ReadyDoc

Live as of today. Both are in **Quality Schedules** — two new tabs:
**Management review** and **Food defense**.

Neither needs setting up. Both schedules are already there, both run once a
year, and both will raise their own task when they come due.

---

## 1. Annual Management Review (SQF 2.1.2.1)

One task, once a year. Completing the task is what files the record — there is
no second form to fill in afterwards.

The task walks all eight items the SQF clause lists, word for word:

1. Food safety policy
2. Food safety plan and its validation
3. Verification activities and their results
4. Internal and external audit findings
5. Corrective actions and their close-out
6. Recalls and regulatory issues
7. Customer complaints and their resolution
8. Follow-up on actions from the previous review

Each item is marked **Reviewed** or **Not applicable with a reason**. There is
no third answer, because the clause says the review covers all eight — so a
review that skips one has to say why, on the record.

It also records who took part, and there is a notes box for anything worth
writing down.

The record is stamped **DRAFT-1**. There is no numbered form for it yet — see
the note at the bottom.

## 2. Annual Food Defense Plan Challenge (SOP 434 V3)

Same shape. One task a year, walking the nine steps in Section 5.0 of the Food
Defense Plan, each tagged with the section it comes from (5.1 B through 5.3).

The five test methods in 5.2 C are offered as buttons to tap. **At least one has
to be used** — that is the only hard "must" in the procedure, so it is the only
thing the app refuses to let through.

It also records the team, what happened, how long people took to respond, the
findings, and any corrective actions raised.

These records carry **SOP 434 V3**, not a draft stamp, because the procedure
behind them is a controlled document.

---

## Recording the challenge already done in March

On the Food defense tab there is a **Record one that already happened** button.
Use that for the March 2026 challenge.

It takes the real date it was done and marks the record as coming from paper, so
it never looks like one the app watched happen live. It asks for exactly the same
things a live one does — a back-filed record cannot be thinner than a fresh one.

Have to hand:

- the date it was run
- who was on the team
- which of the five methods were used
- what happened, and the response times
- the findings
- any corrective actions that came out of it

---

## Two things it does not claim

**Section 5.2 D — rating each mitigation strategy.** The plan's list of
strategies is not in ReadyDoc, so the ratings go in the findings box as written
notes rather than as a tick per strategy. That is honest rather than a grid with
nothing behind it.

**Neither record form has a number.** Nothing in the Master Index covers either
one. Both are with Document Control to number, and the app says so on screen
until they do.
