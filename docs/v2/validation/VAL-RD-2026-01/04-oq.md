# Operational qualification — VAL-RD-2026-01-OQ

**Purpose:** show that each GMP-critical function works as required, in the live app, on the release under test.
**Executed by:** Daniela Servin (test executor, plan §4). Lowry Akers (system administrator) supports — creates the test
accounts and opens dashboards — but does not execute or sign test steps. **Time:** about 1½ hours.

**Test accounts:** `TEST-VAL User` and `TEST-VAL User 2`, operators, created for this OQ and deactivated in OQ-15.

**Test records:** everything created here is named **`TEST-VAL`** so anyone can see what it is. Nothing is deleted
afterwards: the records and their audit trail are the evidence. OQ-15 retires them.

**Automated evidence:** most tests also run automatically against a fresh test database (`npm run verify:part11`,
`verify:sessions`, `verify:accesssources`, `verify:useractivity`, and `verify-signatures.mjs`, `verify-auth.mjs` inside
`verify:all`). They are listed
per test and recorded in IQ-08. The live-app steps below are the ones a person signs for.

How to record a step: Pass or Fail, with your initials and the date. On a Fail, write what you saw, stop the test, and
add a deviation to the summary report.
