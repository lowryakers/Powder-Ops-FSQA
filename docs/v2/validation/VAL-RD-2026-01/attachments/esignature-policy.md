# Electronic signature policy

**Draft 1 — pending Document Control.** Set up in ReadyDoc as training course **ESIG-101**: everyone reads it and passes
a five-question check. Retrained yearly.

**Purpose:** to make ReadyDoc signatures mean the same as handwritten ones (21 CFR Part 11).

1. **Your password is your signature.** When you enter it in ReadyDoc to approve, verify, sign off or record
   something, it means the same as signing your name by hand: **you personally did, reviewed or approved what the
   screen says.**
2. **Your password is yours alone.** Never share it. Never let anyone sign in as you, never sign in for someone else,
   and never write it where others can see it. Nobody from the company, IT included, will ever ask for it.
3. **Signing means what the screen says.** *Approved*: the document is correct and in force. *Trained and competent*:
   you trained this person and they understand. *Verified* / *Reviewed*: you checked the record. Read before you sign.
4. **If you think someone knows your password,** change it straight away (Account menu → Change password) and tell
   your supervisor or QA.
5. **A signature cannot be removed or edited.** A mistake is corrected by a new signed entry with a reason. The
   original stays on the record.
6. **Using someone else's account, or letting someone use yours, is a serious violation** of company policy.

**Owner:** Quality. **Applies to:** everyone with a ReadyDoc account.
