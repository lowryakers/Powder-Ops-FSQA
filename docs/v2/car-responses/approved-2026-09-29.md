## What changed between the draft and what was submitted

Legend: **kept** — the submitted text is the draft, lightly edited · **narrowed** — a commitment the
draft made was dropped · **changed** — the plan itself is different · **new** — a commitment the draft
did not contain.

### 4990683-1 · 4.2.9 · Street shoes — *kept, narrowed, one new fact*
- **New fact in the root cause:** *"At some point shoe covers were bought but the holder was never
  installed."* Better than the draft (a real cause, not a missing rule).
- **Kept:** shoe covers at the entrance; SOP 401 → V5 through a DCR naming the rule, who it covers
  (employees, contractors, visitors) and how it is checked; retraining recorded; **visitor sign-in
  agreement updated with the footwear rule**; weekly GMP walk-through covering gowning, hairnets,
  jewelry, handwashing and footwear with a dated signed record.
- **Narrowed:** the walk is done by *"shift supervisor"* (draft: Maria or a shift supervisor); the
  two-consecutive-misses-raise-a-CAR mechanism, the Form 403-01 footwear question and the
  retrain-on-revision trigger were all dropped. The mechanism still exists (D-060); it is just not
  promised.
- **Responsible moved** from Adam / Daniela / Maria to Carol and Maria.

### 4990683-2 · 4.3.1 · Supplier questionnaires — *kept, narrowed*
- **Kept:** questionnaires requested from the three; Quality dispositions each on return; the register
  with 75 suppliers and 938 documents; the signed-link FORM 404-1 V2; status from documents only; the
  annual-review task 30 days ahead; the three-day Quality + Purchasing reminder; certificate expiry

- **Narrowed:** the counts (21 awaiting disposition, 22 with no questionnaire) and every target date

### 4990683-3 · 4.3.6 · Specifications & identity testing — *changed: much shorter, and a date it cannot meet*
- **Root cause changed in one detail:** limits were *"written in vendor COA"* (draft: in our procedures).
- **Corrective action reduced to three lines:** finish written specifications; identity testing for each

- **Completion: 30 September 2026, Maria alone.** All specifications finished and identity testing in
  place by today. The gate reads 10 lots released with gaps as of 25 Sep (OBL-37); the specifications as

### 4990683-5 · 4.4.39 · Electronic records not validated — *changed: a new SOP, and a date already past*

## Two corrections to keep in mind when the next visit comes

- **Names.** The drafts named the people who do the work (Adam for the entrance control, Daniela for
  every document revision, Ricardo for qualification, Matt for formula content, Juan for receiving).
  The submitted responses name Carol on eleven of twelve and Maria on eight. That is fine for NSF; it is
  not who does the work. The roadmap names the doers.
- **Dates.** Every committed date is September. The auditor will read them as done. Where a thing is not
  done, the honest record is a dated note on the CAR in NSF Connect *before* the visit, not a surprise
  during it — D-056's own rule (a returned response is answered by adding a dated reply, never by
  overwriting).
