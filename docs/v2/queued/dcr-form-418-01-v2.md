# DCR draft — FORM 418-01 V2: QA Film/Pouch/Bottle Sleeve Inspection Checklist

**For:** Document Control (Daniela Servin) · **Raised:** 10 Nov 2026 · **Decision record:** D-332

## Why
QA is starting the bottling line's packaging inspections. A bottle's printed packaging is a shrink sleeve, and
FORM 418-01 V1 has only a FILM section and a POUCH section. ReadyDoc now holds V2 in code and keeps serving V1 until
it is approved in **Controlled Changes** (Document Control nav).

## What changes from V1
1. **Title:** "QA Film/Pouch Inspection Checklist" → "QA Film/Pouch/Bottle Sleeve Inspection Checklist".
2. **New header line: "What is inspected on this sheet"** — Film (rolls) / Pouches / Bottle sleeves. A sheet asks only
   the section for what it inspects. **V1 asks both sections on every sheet** (a film delivery answers the pouch lines
   N/A). Please confirm one section per sheet is how QA works.
3. **Two new header values, bottle sleeves only:** Sleeve lay-flat width (mm), Sleeve cut length (mm).
4. **New section — BOTTLE SLEEVE Inspection** (*Pull 3 sleeves from 4 random rolls or boxes per pallet):
   - Roll tightness acceptable (not loose/crushed) — N/A if delivered as cut sleeves
   - Damage to cores, boxes or outer wrap
   - Eye Mark / cut mark - present and consistent
   - Dimensions - lay-flat width matches specification
   - Dimensions - cut length matches specification
   - Seam - straight, fully sealed, no gaps or solvent marks
   - Perforation / tamper band - present and correct — N/A if the specification has none
   - Artwork - Text and logos centered / correct position
   - Artwork - Grammar/spelling correct
   - Artwork - Nutrition Facts Panel correct
   - Artwork - Barcode present and scans to the product's GTIN
   - Color - Only approved artwork colors
   - Color - Hex color matches standard
   - Color - Visible impression variance
   - Material - Type and thickness match specification (*e.g. PETG, microns, as on the PO / spec)
   - Material - Discoloration or bubbles
   - Material - Wrinkles, scratches or surface defects
   - Material - Feel / texture acceptable
   - Material - Unusual odor present
   - Material - Dust, oil, or foreign material present
   - Material - Pinholes or thin spots (*Flashlight Test if applicable)
   - Shrink test - sleeve applied to a sample bottle shrinks evenly, no distortion of text or NFP

The FILM and POUCH lines are unchanged, typos included.
