# DCR draft — FORM 418-01 V2: QA Film/Pouch/Bottle Sleeve/Bottle Inspection Checklist

**For:** Document Control (Daniela Servin) · **Raised:** 10 Nov 2026 · **Decision records:** D-332, D-348 (bottles added 12 Nov 2026)

## Why
QA is starting the bottling line's packaging inspections. A bottle's printed packaging is a shrink sleeve, and
FORM 418-01 V1 has only a FILM section and a POUCH section. ReadyDoc now holds V2 in code and keeps serving V1 until
it is approved in **Controlled Changes** (Document Control nav).

## What changes from V1
1. **Title:** "QA Film/Pouch Inspection Checklist" → "QA Film/Pouch/Bottle Sleeve/Bottle Inspection Checklist". A shorter title such as "QA Packaging Inspection Checklist" is your call.
2. **New header line: "What is inspected on this sheet"** — Film (rolls) / Pouches / Bottle sleeves / Bottles. A sheet asks only
   the section for what it inspects. **V1 asks both sections on every sheet** (a film delivery answers the pouch lines
   N/A). Please confirm one section per sheet is how QA works.
3. **Two new header values, bottle sleeves only:** Sleeve lay-flat width (mm), Sleeve cut length (mm).
4. **New section — BOTTLE SLEEVE Inspection** (*Pull 3 sleeves from 4 random rolls or boxes per pallet):
   - Roll tightness acceptable (not loose/crushed) — N/A if delivered as cut sleeves
   - Damage to cores, boxes or outer wrap
   - Eye Mark / cut mark - present and consistent
   - Dimensions - lay-flat width matches specification
   - Dimensions - cut length matches specification
   - Seam - straight, fully sealed, no gaps or solvent marks
   - Perforation / tamper band - present and correct — N/A if the specification has none
   - Artwork - Text and logos centered / correct position
   - Artwork - Grammar/spelling correct
   - Artwork - Nutrition Facts Panel correct
   - Artwork - Barcode present and scans to the product's GTIN
   - Color - Only approved artwork colors
   - Color - Hex color matches standard
   - Color - Visible impression variance
   - Material - Type and thickness match specification (*e.g. PETG, microns, as on the PO / spec)
   - Material - Discoloration or bubbles
   - Material - Wrinkles, scratches or surface defects
   - Material - Feel / texture acceptable
   - Material - Unusual odor present
   - Material - Dust, oil, or foreign material present
   - Material - Pinholes or thin spots (*Flashlight Test if applicable)
   - Shrink test - sleeve applied to a sample bottle shrinks evenly, no distortion of text or NFP
5. **Four new header values, bottles only (added 12 Nov, D-348):** Bottle height (mm), Bottle outside diameter (mm),
   Bottle capacity (mL), Neck finish (e.g. 38-400).
6. **New section — BOTTLE Inspection** (*Pull 3 bottles from 4 random cases per pallet) — the empty bottles
   themselves, a food-contact container:
   - Damage to cases, liners or pallet wrap
   - Case labels / lot numbers match the PO
   - Dimensions - height matches specification
   - Dimensions - outside diameter matches specification
   - Dimensions - capacity matches specification
   - Neck finish / threads - correct size, fully formed, no flash
   - Closure fit - a sample cap threads on fully and seals — N/A if no closures on hand
   - Foreign material inside the bottle (dust, plastic shards, insects, debris) (*Invert and look inside each sample)
   - Dust, oil, or foreign material on the outside
   - Cracks, chips, holes or stress whitening
   - Base - flat and stable (no rocker bottom)
   - Mold seams / parting lines - no sharp flash
   - Wall thickness even, no thin spots (*Flashlight Test if applicable)
   - Color / clarity matches standard
   - Material - Type matches specification (*e.g. HDPE, PET, as on the PO / spec)
   - Label / sleeve area smooth, no dents or deformation
   - Unusual odor present

The FILM and POUCH lines are unchanged, typos included.

## Questions for Document Control / QA
- These sleeve lines were **drafted**, not copied from a paper form. Edit freely before issuing; the app follows the
  approved text.
- Is the shrink test done on receipt, or on the first production run?
- Bottles: do closures (caps) arrive on their own pallet and need their own section, or is the closure-fit line enough?
- Bottles: SPEC-BOTTLE has no dimensions yet (vendor, material, closure are listed as unknown). The height, diameter,
  capacity and neck finish the inspector records should be checked against a bottle spec — who supplies those numbers?
