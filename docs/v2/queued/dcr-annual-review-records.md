# DCR — two annual review records that have no form number

**Raised:** 2026-09-29 · **For:** Document Control (Daniela Servin) · **Status:** drafted, not issued

ReadyDoc now files two annual records that the plant's own system requires and that
**no numbered form covers**. Both are live and in use; this asks Document Control to
decide whether each gets a number, and to check the wording against the source.

---

## 1. Annual Management Review record — SQF Code 2.1.2.1

**Source:** SQF Code edition 9, clause 2.1.2.1. The clause names eight things the review
"shall include"; ReadyDoc transcribes all eight verbatim and records each as *reviewed*
or *not applicable with a reason*, plus who took part.

**Stamped `DRAFT-1` on every record**, and every screen says so, because the clause names
a review, not a form. If Document Control issues a number, the code takes it,
`controlled.js` parks the change, and DRAFT-1 records are never re-stamped
(the D-052 shipping-checklist arrangement).

**What to decide:**
1. Does this record get a form number, or is the SQF clause enough on its own?
2. The eight items are the clause's own words. **Do not tidy them** — an auditor reading
   the record beside the code has to find the same eight.
3. Is "not applicable, with a reason" acceptable for an item the clause requires?
   ReadyDoc allows it and demands the reason; it refuses a blank and refuses an
   unexplained N/A.

## 2. Annual Food Defense Plan Challenge record — SOP 434 V3 § 5.0

**Source:** SOP 434 V3 (Food Defense Plan, author Carol Pierce, effective 11 Sep 2026),
Section 5.0. The nine steps ReadyDoc records are transcribed from § 5.1 – § 5.3, each
tagged with its clause. § 5.2 C's five challenge methods are offered as they are written,
and at least one must be used — the procedure's only "must".

**Records carry `SOP 434 V3`, NOT a draft stamp**, because the procedure itself is
controlled. What is not numbered is the **Annual Food Defense Plan Challenge Report**
that § 6.0 names as a record.

**What to decide:**
1. Does the Annual Challenge Report get a form number? § 6.0 requires the record and the
   Master Index has none.
2. § 5.2 D asks for **each mitigation strategy** to be rated Effective / Partially
   Effective / Ineffective. ReadyDoc records the ratings as written findings against
   § 5.2 D rather than a per-strategy grid, because the Plan's strategy list is not in
   ReadyDoc. If the ratings should be structured per strategy, the strategy list has to
   come from the Plan first.
3. § 5.1 A ("schedule at least 30 days before the anniversary") is a scheduling
   instruction, not a record, so it is not one of the nine ticks. The annual schedule
   raises its task on the anniversary; if the 30-day lead should be enforced, say so.

## Both

- Records are filed by **completing the annual task** in the Task Center — one act.
- A review that already happened can be **recorded after the fact**, with its own date,
  stamped `source: 'paper'` so it never reads as one ReadyDoc watched happen. The Food
  Defense challenge of **March 2026** is expected to be entered this way.
- Neither record can be filed half-answered, from either door.
