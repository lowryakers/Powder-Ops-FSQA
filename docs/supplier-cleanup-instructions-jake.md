# Suppliers in ReadyDoc — what to do, in order

For Jake Waits, Purchasing Manager. Written 2 November 2026.

Open **ReadyDoc → Suppliers**. If a supplier doesn't show **Edit details** or **Attach documents** when you open it,
ask Lowry to give you **Suppliers: Edit** in Settings.

You never approve a supplier. Once the documents are in, Maria or Carol reviews each one and records the decision.

---

## Step 1 — The three suppliers NSF named (do these first)

NSF found no supplier questionnaire on file for **Mill Haven Foods**, **M4 Dynamic** and **Bay State Milling**. That
finding is still open.

For each of the three:

1. Open the supplier and press **Send questionnaire link**.
2. Email the link to their quality contact. They fill in FORM 404-1 and sign it online, and it files itself.
3. If no quality contact is listed, ask them who it is. Add the person with **Add contact**, role **Quality / food
   safety**, and tick **Main contact**.
4. Chase until all three are back.

## Step 2 — Suppliers we are buying from without approval

Open the **Needs attention** tab. The top list is **Buying without qualification**. Every supplier on it is one we
order from with no approval.

- **"Have no questionnaire to decide against"** — these are yours. Send each one a questionnaire link, as in Step 1.
- **"Can be decided now"** — these already have evidence on file and are waiting on Quality. Nothing to do.
- If a supplier on the list is one **we no longer buy from**, open it, press **Edit details**, untick **We still buy
  from this supplier**, and say why (for example "Switched to Pouch Co in March 2026"). It leaves the list.

## Step 3 — Letters of guarantee (packaging and food-contact suppliers)

1. On every supplier that sells us something that touches the product (bags, liners, film, gloves, scoops), tick
   **Supplies food-contact material**. Packaging suppliers are counted automatically.
2. Ask each one for their letter of guarantee (a continuing guarantee or certificate of compliance for food contact).
3. Upload it with **Attach letter of guarantee**. Enter the **renewal date** first; it won't save without one.

The register's **Letter of guarantee** column, and the count at the top, show who still owes one.

## Step 4 — Expired and expiring documents

On **Needs attention**, work the **Expired documents** list and then **Expiring within 120 days**. Ask each
supplier for a current copy and upload it with **Attach documents**.

## Step 5 — Clean up the rest of the list

Go through the **Register** one supplier at a time. Open each and press **Edit details**:

- **We still buy from this supplier** — untick it for anyone we no longer order from, with the reason.
- **Type** — set it for every supplier (Ingredient, Packaging, Supplies, Equipment, Service, Laboratory). This
  matters: a packaging supplier with the wrong type won't be asked for a letter of guarantee.
- **Website, address, notes** — fill in what you know.
- **Contacts** — add the quality or food safety contact for each supplier, with their role. Fix or remove contacts
  that are wrong.

If the same company is listed twice, or a supplier was added that we never used, send it to Lowry. Don't create
another entry to fix it.

## Step 6 — Food safety certificates (as they come in)

If a supplier has an SQF, BRC, FSSC 22000 or NSF certificate, ask for a copy and upload it with **Attach documents**.

## How to name a file before you upload it

ReadyDoc works out what a document is, and when it expires, from the file name. Name files like this:

- `Vendor Questionnaire 2026.pdf`
- `Letter of Guarantee 2026.pdf`
- `SQF Certificate Exp 12.31.2026.pdf`
- `Kosher Certificate Exp 06.30.2027.pdf`

A file called `scan001.pdf` is stored, but nobody will know what it is or when it runs out, and it can't be
re-labelled afterwards.
