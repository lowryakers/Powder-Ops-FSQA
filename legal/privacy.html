Powder Ops · ReadyDoc

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 July 2026

ReadyDoc is a private, internal application built and operated by Powder Ops LLC ("Powder Ops", "we") for its own employees and contractors. It is not offered to the public, and accounts are created only by a Powder Ops administrator. This policy explains what the application stores, why, and who it is shared with.

Who this covers

Everyone who signs in to ReadyDoc: Powder Ops employees, contractors, and any auditor or customer representative given temporary access.

What we store

CategoryExamplesWhy
Account Name, sign-in username, role, department, password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash), session tokens To sign you in and decide what you can see

We do not collect location data, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell or rent any data to anyone.

QuickBooks Online

Where Powder Ops connects ReadyDoc to its own QuickBooks Online company, the application reads bills and invoices — vendor and customer names, invoice and PO numbers, dates, amounts and balances — and copies them into the internal accounts payable and accounts receivable ledgers so the office works in one place.

  • The connection is to Powder Ops' own company file. We never access anyone else's.
  • Access is read-only: ReadyDoc does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks.
  • Access and refresh tokens are stored on our server and are used only to fetch that data.
  • You can disconnect at any time — see Disconnecting QuickBooks.

Service providers

Data is processed by a small number of providers, each only for the function listed:

  • Railway — application hosting and the database.
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage of uploaded files and backups.
  • Anthropic — optional AI features (translation, document proofreading, question answering). Content sent for these features is not used to train models.
  • Voyage AI — optional search embeddings for message search.
  • Twilio — optional SMS for approval requests.
  • Intuit — where the QuickBooks connection above is enabled.

We share data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

Security

  • All traffic is served over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes; nobody, including administrators, can read them.
  • Access is granted per module, per person, by an administrator.
  • Repeated failed sign-ins lock an account temporarily.
  • File downloads use short-lived links issued only after an access check.

How long we keep it

Food-safety and quality records are retained for at least the period our certification scheme and customer agreements require (generally a minimum of two years, and longer where a product's shelf life demands it). Accounts are deactivated rather than deleted so historical records keep naming the person who signed them. Messages, files and business records are kept for as long as they are useful to the business.

Your choices

To see what the application holds about you, correct it, or ask about deletion, contact us at the address below. Because much of the data is a regulated food-safety record, we may be unable to delete an entry that a compliance obligation requires us to keep; we will say so plainly if that is the case.

Text messages (SMS)

ReadyDoc can send text messages to staff and to named business contacts — an approval link for a flavour or nutrition-panel decision, and answers to questions an authorised person texts to our number. It is a workplace tool: we do not send marketing or promotional text messages, and there is no public sign-up.

We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers with anyone for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers collected for text messaging are used only to send the messages described above, and are shared only with our messaging provider (Twilio) for the sole purpose of delivering them. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional use.

Consent. A number is added to ReadyDoc by an administrator only after the person it belongs to has agreed to receive these messages, and the application records who recorded that agreement and when. Consent is not a condition of employment or of any purchase.

Message frequency. Messages are transactional and occasional — typically fewer than ten per month per recipient, and only when a decision is genuinely waiting or the recipient has texted us a question. Frequency varies with plant activity.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time; reply HELP for help. Opting out stops all further messages to that number. You can also ask an administrator to remove your number from ReadyDoc entirely.

Children

ReadyDoc is a workplace tool and is not directed to anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and staff are notified in the application.
