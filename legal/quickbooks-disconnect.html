Disconnecting QuickBooks

Last updated 27 July 2026

ReadyDoc connects to the Powder Ops QuickBooks Online company to copy bills and invoices into its internal accounts payable and accounts receivable ledgers. The connection is read-only — ReadyDoc never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks.

Disconnect from QuickBooks

In QuickBooks Online, open Settings (gear icon) → Apps, find ReadyDoc, and choose Disconnect. Access ends immediately.

Disconnect from ReadyDoc

An administrator can also end the connection from our side by removing the QuickBooks credentials from the application's environment settings. Syncing stops at once and the stored refresh token is no longer used.

What happens to data already copied

Invoices already copied into the accounts payable and receivable ledgers stay there, as they form part of Powder Ops' own business records. No further data is fetched after disconnection. To have copied records removed as well, contact us at the address below.
